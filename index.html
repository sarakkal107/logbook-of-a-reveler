In Honor of Jinesh KJ

Thank you for visiting my logbook! This project was created to remember Jinesh KJ (Kunju Mama)'s legacy and the impact he had on those around him. Kunju Mama tragically lost his battle to Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia in 2011 at the age of 25, and this project serves as a tribute to his memory.

Inspiration: Logbook of an Observer by Jinesh KJ
A posthumous collection of writings.

About This Project

Kunju Mama was a vibrant soul who brought joy and laughter to everyone he met who left behind a legacy of love and inspiration. I remember him for his infectious smile and his long hair. Due to circumstance, I was not able to get to know him as closely as I would have liked; I was only 10 years old when he passed away.

I recently celebrated my 25th birthday, and I wanted to do something special to honor his memory. This year, I plan on completing 25 items as a remembrance ritual to commemorate his life. This serves as a reminder that life is precious and that we should cherish every moment we have with our loved ones. I hope that this project serves as a source of inspiration and comfort to those who knew him, and to those who are just discovering his story.

Completion Target Date: September 29th, 2027
